**SUU** is a **multi-purpose Android utility** focused on **storage optimization**, keeping storage cleaner while strengthening the overall experience through other features

<Callout type='idea' title='Not limited to root'>
  **Standard permissions**, **ADB** and **DeviceOwner** can all drive parts of **SUU**, even though not every feature is
  available
  <></>
  **Xposed injection** can unlock more features and broaden usage scenarios
</Callout>

The **Windows version** of **SUU** can also serve as a companion that further enhances Android tinkering, extending the same goal from **Android itself** to **Windows**

The **web version** of **SUU** was split out from [**ShiroSU NT**](/newtech). Alongside its flashing workflow, it also provides more Android tinkering features

Its web tools use **WebUSB** to connect through **ADB or fastboot** for a range of operations, without needing to install any programs locally

> ADB connections usually require no drivers; fastboot requires compatible drivers to be installed
