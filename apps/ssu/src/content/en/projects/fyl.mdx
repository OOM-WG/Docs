**ShiroSU FYL** is a development library split out from [**ShiroSU Compat**](/compat) and [**SUU**](/utils), exposing parts of their internal implementations

**ShiroSU FYL** consists of multiple subfeatures and focuses on providing capable implementations across different scenarios
