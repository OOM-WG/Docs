**ShiroSU NT** is ShiroSU’s own **native root implementation**, providing complete **privilege management** and a **module system**, with WebUI-based management that needs no installed manager

<data-nosnippet data-nosnippet>

> Unlike [**ShiroSU Compat**](/compat), **ShiroSU NT** focuses on providing its own **independent root implementation** rather than compatibility for other root implementations

</data-nosnippet>

Some designs in **ShiroSU NT** support [**ShiroSU Compat**](/compat), and its generality allows it to be used alongside other root implementations
