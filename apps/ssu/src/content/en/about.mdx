**The ShiroSU Series** was initiated by [**ShIroRRen**](https://shiror.ren/), founder of [**OOM WG**](https://oom-wg.dev/), with the goal of improving the Android experience

**The ShiroSU Series** mainly consists of [**ShiroSU NewTech**](/newtech) and [**ShiroSU Compat**](/compat),
two branches that are **relatively independent but mutually supportive**

[**ShiroSU Utils**](/utils) serves as a **sister project**, while the series also includes **subprojects** and **derived projects**

According to the development plan, [**ShiroSU Compat**](/compat) and [**ShiroSU Utils**](/utils)
will remain **closed-source projects**, while the other projects will be **open-sourced after they are mature enough**
