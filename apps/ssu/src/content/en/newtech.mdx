**ShiroSU NT** is ShiroSU's own **native root implementation**, providing full **privilege management** and **module system** capabilities

> Unlike [**ShiroSU Compat**](/compat),
> **ShiroSU NT** focuses on providing its own **independent root implementation** instead of compatibility layers for other root implementations

Some designs in **ShiroSU NT** support [**ShiroSU Compat**](/compat),
and it also provides a **module builder** for **ShiroSU NT** and other root implementations
