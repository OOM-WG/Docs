**ShiroSU&trade;** is a project series initiated by [**ShIroRRen**](https://shiror.ren/), founder of [**OOM WG**](https://oom-wg.dev/), to improve the Android experience

The series is mainly composed of [**ShiroSU NewTech**](/newtech) and [**ShiroSU Compat**](/compat), two **relatively independent yet mutually supportive** branches

[**ShiroSU Utils**](/utils) exists as a **sister project**, while several [**supporting projects**](/projects#sub) also contribute to the series

> According to the development plan, [**ShiroSU Compat**](/compat) and [**ShiroSU Utils**](/utils) will remain **closed-source**, while other closed-source projects will be released after they are sufficiently complete
